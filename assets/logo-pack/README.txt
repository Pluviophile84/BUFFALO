LIMITS
  - The bison and the ground are embedded images inside the SVG. With the coin edge to edge, they stay fully sharp
    up to about 1218 px wide on screen, or about 10.3 cm wide in print at 300 dpi. Anything larger (merch, banners,
    large print) needs a fully vector version of the bison.
  - Below 32 px the B's crossbar and the bison's detail disappear. That's normal at that size; the coin and the B still read.
  - The binary line under the ground spells |BUFFALO⟩ in ASCII binary and is legible from about 1000 px up.
